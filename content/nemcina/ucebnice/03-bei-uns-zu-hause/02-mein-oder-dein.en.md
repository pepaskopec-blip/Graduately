# Překlady

| Česky | Anglicky |
|---|---|
| Je to tvoje matka? – Ne, to není moje matka. To je jeho teta. | Is that your mother? – No, that is not my mother. That is his aunt. |
| Je to tvůj bratr? – Ne, to není můj bratr. To je jeho bratranec. | Is that your brother? – No, that is not my brother. That is his cousin. |
| Jsou to tvoji rodiče? – Ne, to nejsou moji rodiče. To jsou rodiče Markuse. | Are those your parents? – No, those are not my parents. Those are Markus's parents. |
