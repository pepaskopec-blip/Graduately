# Výklad

## Ne izolovaná slovíčka
*Okruhy*

- Heavy rain, strong coffee, fast food, junk food. Ne strong rain.
- A balanced diet, processed food, a food allergy.
- Public transport, rush hour, a traffic jam, a return ticket.
- Renewable energy, a carbon footprint, a heatwave, a drought.

> Tip: ústní zkouška sahá na osobu, rodinu, domov, školu, volný čas, kulturu, sport, cestování, zdraví, jídlo, služby, práci, společnost a přírodu.

## Fráze, které unesou odstavec
*Společnost a práce*

- A gap year, an apprenticeship, a degree, tuition fees.
- Freedom of speech, a stereotype, volunteer work.
- Break the law, pay a fine, a witness.
- Mental health, a check-up, be in pain. Ne have pain in the leg jako jediná možnost: My leg hurts.

> Tip: apply for a job, hand in a notice, earn a living, from home.


# Doplňování

## There was heavy ___ all night.
= rain
> Heavy rain, ne strong rain.

## I'd like a ___ ticket to Brno.
= return
> Return ticket = zpáteční.

## She applied ___ the post of guide.
= for
> Apply for.

## Solar power is ___ energy.
= renewable
> Renewable energy.

## He has a nut ___, so he can't eat the cake.
= allergy
> A food allergy.


# Kvíz

## Which pair is right?
- strong rain / heavy coffee
- [x] heavy rain / strong coffee
- fast coffee / junk rain
- a traffic diet
> Heavy rain, strong coffee.

## Rush hour is…
- a quiet morning
- [x] the busiest time to travel
- a kind of ticket
- a school subject
> Špička.

## An apprenticeship is…
- a fine
- [x] training for a job
- a heatwave
- a stereotype
> Praktická příprava na řemeslo nebo práci.

## My leg ___.
- [x] hurts
- is pain
- has hurted
- does hurtness
> My leg hurts.
