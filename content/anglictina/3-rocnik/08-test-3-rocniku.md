# Výklad

## Trpný rod, řeč, podmínky, dedukce
*B1+*

- was written, must be signed, is spoken.
- said she would, asked where I lived.
- If they banned cars, the air would be cleaner.
- must have, might have, can't have.

> Tip: u trpného rodu hledejte be. U nepřímé otázky žádnou inverzi.

## However a because v jednom textu
*Úvaha ve zkratce*

- Neopakujte zadání první větou.
- Phrasal verbs nechte ve správné částici. Look after není look for.
- Told me, ne said me.
- In conclusion je až na konci, ne v úvodu.

> Tip: jedna výhoda, jeden háček, jedna věta s názorem.


# Čtení

A repair café opened in a former bakery. Broken radios are mended there on Saturdays. The founder said that people would rather repair than buy if they were shown how. She told a reporter that twenty kettles had been saved in March. Visitors have to bring the broken object, but they don't have to pay. If the café closed, a lot of working parts would be thrown away.

## What happens to radios there?
- They are sold
- [x] They are mended
- They are banned
- They are thrown away at once
> are mended.

## How many kettles had been saved in March?
- Twelve
- [x] Twenty
- Two thousand
- None
> twenty kettles.

## What would happen if the café closed?
- [x] Parts would be thrown away
- Radios would be cheaper
- People would pay more
- The bakery would reopen as a shop
> working parts would be thrown away.


# Poslech

Editor: Did the mayor answer you? Journalist: I asked him whether the bridge was safe. He said he didn't know. I also asked how long the repairs would take. He told me that the firm hadn't decided. A resident asked me why the paper hadn't printed a map. I said we would print one tomorrow.

## What did the journalist ask about safety?
- [x] Whether the bridge was safe
- If the paper was closed
- Where the mayor lived
- Why kettles break
> whether the bridge was safe.

## What hadn't the firm done?
- [x] Decided
- Printed a map
- Closed the paper
- Asked the resident
> hadn't decided.

## What did the journalist promise?
- To close the bridge
- [x] To print a map tomorrow
- To repair the cables
- To interview a baker
> we would print one tomorrow.


# Psaní

In at least 40 words, give one reason for repairing things and one objection. Use because and however.

## Vzorová odpověď

Repairing things matters because a kettle can work for years after a small fix. However, some people do not have the tools or the time, so a café where volunteers help is a fair idea. I believe every town should have one quiet room for broken objects. It is slower than shopping, but it is kinder.

## Musí obsahovat

- because
- however

## Minimum slov

40


# Kvíz

## The cake ___ by a student.
- baked
- [x] was baked
- was bake
- has bake
> was baked.

## She asked me where I ___.
- did live
- [x] lived
- do live
- living
> where I lived.

## He ___ be at school. I just saw him there.
- can't
- [x] must
- mustn't
- should to
> Skoro jistota: must.

## Please look ___ the tickets. I can't find them.
- [x] for
- forward
- after
- up of
> Look for = hledat. Look after by bylo hlídat.
