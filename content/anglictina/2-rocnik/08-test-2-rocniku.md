# Výklad

## Co už má držet pohromadě
*B1*

- Stupňování, past continuous, modální slovesa rady a povinnosti.
- Present perfect proti past simple.
- Who a which, too a enough, první a druhá podmínka.
- Psaní: krátký souvislý názor, ne odrážkový seznam.

> Tip: u předpřítomného času hledejte ever, yet, since. U minulého yesterday a rok.

## Jedna věta navíc
*Strategie*

- U čtení škrtněte odpověď, která v textu vůbec není.
- U poslechu si čísla zapište číslicí.
- Mustn't není don't have to.
- If I would have time je chyba. If I had time, I would…

> Tip: v psaní ať je důvod. Because nebo so text zvedne.


# Čtení

The night bus to Brno is cheaper than the train, but it is also slower. Last Friday I was sleeping when the driver stopped at a dark petrol station. A woman who had missed her connection asked if she could sit next to me. She has travelled this route since March because her new job starts at six. If the bus is late, she has to run. She says she would move closer if flats were cheaper.

## The bus is ___ than the train.
- faster and cheaper
- [x] cheaper and slower
- the best
- as fast
> cheaper, but slower.

## What was the writer doing when the bus stopped?
- Reading
- [x] Sleeping
- Running
- Driving
> I was sleeping when the driver stopped.

## Why has the woman travelled this route since March?
- She likes petrol stations
- [x] Her job starts early
- Flats are cheap
- She missed a concert
> her new job starts at six.


# Poslech

Doctor: How long have you had this cough? Adam: Since Sunday. I have also had a sore throat for two days. Doctor: You should rest and drink water. You don't have to stay in bed all day, but you mustn't play football this week. If you still feel ill on Friday, come back.

## Since when has Adam had the cough?
- Since Friday
- [x] Since Sunday
- For a month
- Since March
> Since Sunday.

## What mustn't he do?
- Drink water
- Rest
- [x] Play football
- Come back
> mustn't play football.

## When should he come back?
- [x] If he is still ill on Friday
- Every day
- Never
- On Sunday morning
> If you still feel ill on Friday.


# Psaní

Write at least 35 words about a trip or a job you would like. Use would and because.

## Vzorová odpověď

I would like to work in a small bookshop by the station because I love quiet mornings. I would talk to customers and I would also read in the breaks. If I had the job, I would cycle there. I have never worked in a shop, but I have helped at school events.

## Musí obsahovat

- would
- because

## Minimum slov

35


# Kvíz

## This hostel is ___ than the hotel.
- [x] cheaper
- more cheap
- the cheapest
- cheap
> cheap → cheaper.

## I have lived here ___ 2020.
- for
- [x] since
- ago
- during
> Since + bod v čase.

## You ___ wear a helmet. It's the law.
- must to
- [x] have to
- don't have
- should to
> Pravidlo: have to.

## If I ___ richer, I would take the train.
- am
- will be
- [x] were
- would be
> 2. podmínka: were.
