# Výklad

## Příliš a dost
*Too / enough*

- It's too cold to swim. The water isn't warm enough.
- Too many people, too much rain.
- Enough + podstatné jméno: enough water. Nebo za přídavným jménem: old enough.
- Not enough time je častější než too little time.

> Tip: too stojí před přídavným jménem, enough za ním.

## Co říct, když se díváte z okna
*Předpověď*

- Those clouds are black. It's going to storm.
- I think it will be sunny on Sunday. To je jen odhad.
- If it rains, we'll stay in the cabin.
- Slovník: foggy, chilly, pouring, a heatwave, a breeze.

> Tip: mraky, které vidíte, vedou k going to. Odhad bez důkazu k will.


# Čtení

We wanted to cycle to the lake, but the morning was too foggy to start. By ten the sun was out and the path was dry enough. There were too many cyclists near the dam, so we took a quieter road. We didn't have enough water, which was a mistake. At the lake a guard said, "Look at that cloud. It's going to pour. I think the storm will last an hour." We were not fast enough. We got soaked.

## Why didn't they start early?
- The bikes were broken

> too foggy to start.

## Why did they change the road?
- [x] There were too many cyclists
- They were lost
- It was pouring
- The guard sent them back
> too many cyclists near the dam.

## What mistake did they make?
- They forgot the bikes
- [x] They didn't have enough water
- They started at night
- They swam
> didn't have enough water.

## What did the guard predict from the cloud?
- Fog
- A heatwave
- [x] Rain
- Snow
> It's going to pour.


# Doplňování

## The rucksack is ___ heavy to carry.
= too
> Too + přídavné jméno.

## She isn't tall ___ to reach the shelf.
= enough
> Enough za přídavným jménem.

## There is too ___ sugar in this tea.
= much
> Nepočitatelné: too much.

## Look at the sky. It ___ snow.
= is going to|'s going to
> Viditelný důkaz.
